# Decision poster key

Read after submitting a first attempt. The case is fictional.

- **Meals and geometry.** The requirement is 87 × 240 + 1,000 = 21,880 mL, so 15 trays provide the minimum 22,500 mL. There are 1,620 mL left after serving reservations, but only 620 mL may be used without touching the reserve. Two full extra portions fit. The 24 m² room minus a 9 m² strip leaves 15 m². At 3 m² each, five is an area upper bound, not a guarantee: footprints, access around each station, and the remaining shape matter. A walk-in list should not count a person already served from the reservation list.
- **Route.** With the lift closed and the passage unverified, G → W → H is the verified step-free path, with a 9-minute estimate. Confirm the ramp and west entrance remain open. The 8-minute estimate is conditional on confirming the passage, and still not an arrival guarantee.
- **Programming.** Art, repair and history get (3, 2, 0) with largest remainder, or (2, 2, 1) with the one-slot minimum and reallocation of unmet requests. The minimum gives history access and costs art one slot. On the original ballots, A wins plurality with 5 first choices; C wins the instant runoff 7–5; B wins both head-to-head comparisons against the other options. Results must be paired with the chosen rule.
- **Weather decision.** Indoors gives $25 with the stated assumptions. The outdoor plan has a $47.50 expectation but fails the $100 state-loss limit: wet weather costs $170 net. A verified wet-only $80 guarantee reduces that loss to $90 and raises the expectation to $67.50. Outdoor then passes the modeled limit and has the larger expectation. Neither average is cash on hand. The sponsor's ability and obligation to pay, and the completeness of the assumed costs and states, matter.
- **Changed inputs.** At a 1,200 mL reserve, 15 trays are still enough but 420 mL is uncommitted, permitting one full extra portion. With the passage now confirmed and the lift still closed, G → J → W → H gives the fastest verified step-free route in the model at exactly 8 minutes. Neither change changes the other decisions. It is still an estimate.
